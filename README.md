# Road-Accident-Dashboard
## 🔍 Overview:
This dashboard analyzes road accident data to understand patterns, causes, and severity levels across various regions and conditions. It aims to help decision-makers in traffic management, policy enforcement, and safety improvements.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
These are the core metrics highlighted in the dashboard:

- Total Number of Accidents

- Accidents by Severity (e.g., Fatal, Serious, Slight)

- Accidents by Region or State

- Accidents by Time Period (monthly/yearly trend)

- Weather or Road Condition Contribution

- Casualties (Injured and Deceased)

## 💡 Key Insights:
**1.Accident Trends Over Time:**

- A clear peak in accidents during specific months or seasons.

- Weekend or evening hours may show higher accident rates.

**2.Severity Distribution:**

- Most accidents are slight, but fatal accidents represent a critical focus due to their impact.

- Certain regions show disproportionately high fatal accidents compared to others.

**3.Geographic Hotspots:**

- Specific cities/regions consistently show higher accident counts.

- These could benefit from targeted traffic reforms or public safety campaigns.

**4.Weather and Road Conditions:**

- Wet or icy roads often correlate with higher accident frequency or severity.

- Poor visibility and rain also contribute significantly.

**4.Vehicle Types Involved:**

- Two-wheelers and commercial vehicles are more involved in fatal or serious accidents.

- Suggests a need for stricter regulations or training.
